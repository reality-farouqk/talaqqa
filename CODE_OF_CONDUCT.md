# Code of Conduct

Talaqqa is a place to learn. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews or discussions) is expected to:

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent and ask before concluding.
- Keep discussion about the project, the code and the content guidelines.
- Speak about teachers and scholars with respect, even when proposing that their content not be included.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, sectarian attacks, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. Report a problem by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer privately.
